Source and style follow-up — October 22, 2026

This records local source changes after the published 1.21.21 release. It does not change that release's build-time verification snapshot or claim a new packaged release.

The requested style scope is comparison with explicitly listed guide forms and supported explicit provisions. A general grammatical interpretation is not independent evidence of a discrepancy. Correct listed wording stays quiet even when its grammatical role has not been established. General modifier, noun-group, competing-parse, meaning and comma/conjunction series questions have been removed from production Stylistic Check. Processing limits no longer highlight complete sentences as style discrepancies. The unlisted data sharing agreement inference is also disabled. Exact listed discrepancies, supported explicit formatting checks and the separate California Codes, Counties and Entities references remain available. Source-form review no longer manufactures unlisted prefix/root or productive-ending candidates. This deliberately reduces automatic coverage of meaning-dependent prose rules; it is not full LCB certification.
